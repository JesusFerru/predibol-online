# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Predibol is used by World Cup pool participants who need to submit predictions, check rankings, review rules, and see their own performance during the tournament.

## Product Purpose

The product manages a private online prediction pool backed by Supabase. Success means users can trust their saved predictions, understand their standing, and see feedback from finished matches without needing manual explanations.

## Brand Personality

Competitive, direct, social.

## Anti-references

Avoid over-designed betting dashboards, confusing casino patterns, and decorative UI that makes scores, deadlines, payments, or rankings harder to verify.

## Design Principles

- Make the source of truth visible when users are checking points, predictions, and payments.
- Prefer simple, scannable flows over elaborate visual treatment.
- Treat unreliable or incomplete data as normal and show graceful fallback states.
- Keep authenticated tournament tasks fast on mobile.
- Design security and access control into the route structure, not as an afterthought.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Aim for clear contrast, readable data labels, keyboard-friendly navigation, and reduced reliance on color alone for chart meaning.
